## Documents

The following PDF can best be printed on A3. It contains 8 different half-noisy
images, with the corresponding prompt. If printed on A3, the little circular
pixels are roughly 8 mm in diameter, which means you can use black and white
sticker marker points to cover them in Assignment 1.

<!--pdfs-->

Provide sheets with these 8 mm adhesive dots in colours black and white. The dots
should cover the pixels in Assignment 1. Every student should get one sheet with
black and one with white stickers.
