# Art exhibition

In this one-day project, students create an exhibition on the theme "The Future:
perspectives of the youth". Using AI and their own creativity, they make the
artworks themselves (such as drawings, poems, music or comics) and everything
around them: the press release, the web page and the posters. The work is
structured with the design thinking framework, so that students stay at the centre
of the creative process and in charge of what the AI does.
