# Debate

Students debate a question about AI, such as whether AI-made works should be
allowed to have copyright. In groups of two or three, they are assigned a thesis
and a side (for or against), research the topic and prepare their arguments. Then
they debate the other side, while the audience prepares questions. The document
below contains the suggested debate topics.
