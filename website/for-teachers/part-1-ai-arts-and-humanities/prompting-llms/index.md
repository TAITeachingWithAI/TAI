# Prompting LLMs

Students first ask an LLM to explain a topic and look critically at the answer.
They then learn tips for writing better prompts, such as asking for sources, and
practise them on example tasks. Throughout the lesson, the message is that
students stay responsible for the answer and should check the facts the LLM gives.

## Lesson material

There is no separate teacher version of this lesson: teachers use the same material as the students.
