# Literature

Students write a personal "literary wish list" with the characters, topics and
writing style they find interesting. The AI then gives them three short texts that
match their wish list. Students compare and analyse the texts, guess when each one
was written and try to spot which one the AI wrote itself, before asking the AI
for the answers and a further analysis.

## Lesson material

There is no separate teacher version of this lesson: teachers use the same material as the students.
