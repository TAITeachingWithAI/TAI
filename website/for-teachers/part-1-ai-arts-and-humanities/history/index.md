# History

In groups of three, students choose a historical figure and a decision that this
figure made, and research its historical context. They then debate the decision
with an AI that plays the historical figure: they ask why the decision was made,
and challenge it. The lesson ends with an analysis and conclusions. Choosing the
figure and the research can be done as preparation, before the lesson.

## Lesson material

There is no separate teacher version of this lesson: teachers use the same material as the students.
