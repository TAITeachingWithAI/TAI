# Art styles

Students learn to recognise art styles and to put them into words, which is
exactly what you need to prompt an image generator. In the first half, they spot
the differences between styles and identify the style of a mystery artwork, using
AI responsibly: describing images, asking for verification links from museums and
cross-checking facts. In the second half, they recreate their mystery image with
prompts, swap prompts with another group to guess each other's images, and design
a logo.
