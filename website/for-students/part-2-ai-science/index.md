# Part 2: AI & Science

In Part 2 you work like a scientist, with AI as your assistant. In most lessons,
your group investigates one of eight real exoplanets: you measure its size, work
out its orbit, analyse a sample collected by a rover and design life that could
survive there. In robotics, you teach a rover to learn by itself. The AI helps
you, but you stay in charge: you check its suggestions and draw your own
conclusions.
