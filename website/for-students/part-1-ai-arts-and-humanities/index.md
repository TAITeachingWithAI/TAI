# Part 1: AI & Arts and Humanities

How does an AI write a text, create an image or make a sound? In Part 1 you find
out how generative AI works, and you use it yourself in languages, literature,
history, art and music. Along the way, you learn to look critically at what AI
produces: what does it do well, and where does it go wrong?
